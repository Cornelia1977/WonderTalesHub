# Blog covers: the brief and the prompts

Every cover is a picture in the app's own world: painted, Pixar-like
animal characters, a night-sky palette (deep navy and purple with warm
gold light), stars and soft glows. No humans, no text, no logos. Each
post gets one cover at 16:10, at least 1600 × 1000 pixels, exported as
WebP or JPEG under 400 KB and uploaded on the post in the admin.

## Style line (paste at the start of every prompt)

> Children's storybook illustration in a polished 3D-painted style, like a
> Pixar film still: a cute animal character with big expressive eyes and
> soft fur, warm golden light against a deep navy and violet night sky,
> glowing stars and tiny fireflies, soft bokeh, gentle dreamy mood,
> rich detail, no people, no text, no watermark, 16:10 landscape.

## One prompt per post

**The Art of Storytelling: How to Read Bedtime Stories**
> [style line] A fluffy fox parent and a small fox cub tucked under a
> starry quilt in a cosy bedroom at night, the parent reading from an open
> storybook that glows warm gold, a crescent moon through the window, a
> bedside lamp with a soft halo, a sleepy teddy bear on the pillow.

**How to Invent a Bedtime Story on the Spot**
> [style line] A tired but smiling raccoon parent sitting on the edge of a
> small bed, conjuring a story from thin air: a swirl of golden sparkles
> rising from its paws into tiny floating pictures of a dragon, a boat and
> a castle, while a wide-eyed rabbit child watches from under the blanket.

**Enchanting Night Tales: the science of bedtime stories**
> [style line] A wise owl in round spectacles at a little wooden desk by a
> round window, surrounded by glowing books, a brass telescope and a small
> globe, one open book projecting a soft galaxy of stars and a sleeping
> crescent moon above, calm and scholarly.

## More, for future posts

**Sleep and screens**
> [style line] A small hedgehog curled up asleep on a cloud-shaped pillow,
> a tablet switched off and set aside on a nightstand, its dark screen
> reflecting the stars, a glowing storybook open beside it instead.

**Family voices, grandparents far away**
> [style line] An old grey bear in a cardigan speaking softly into a
> glowing seashell, and across the picture a bear cub in bed holding a
> matching seashell to its ear, a thread of golden sparkles linking the
> two across a starry sky.

**Reading in two languages**
> [style line] Two otter siblings sharing one big storybook in bed, the
> pages glowing with gentle golden letters that drift up like fireflies
> in two different scripts, a lantern on the nightstand.

**The birthday story**
> [style line] A small deer in a paper crown sitting up in bed, a single
> candle-lit cupcake on the nightstand, a storybook opening by itself
> with golden confetti and stars spilling out, night window behind.

## Checking a cover before upload

- Same palette as the app's theme pictures (`src/assets/app/*.webp`).
- The subject fills the frame; the card crops it to 4:3 on the list page.
- Nothing readable in the picture: Gemini sometimes adds lettering on
  books; regenerate if it does.
